🎉 Installed. Here's the 20-second tour.

  1. Pin it. Click the puzzle-piece 🧩 icon at the top-right of Chrome, then the pin 📌 next to WhatTheF*#%IsPlayingMusicRightNow. Now the button is always visible.
  2. Watch the badge. When any tab in any window makes noise, a red number appears on the icon. Green means only calls are making sound.
  3. Click the icon. See what's playing, hit 👀 Show me to jump to it, or smash PAUSE EVERYTHING.
  4. Keyboard: Alt+Shift+P pauses everything from anywhere. Alt+Shift+F finds the noise.
